# Brief 02 — Brand, Assets and Content Contracts

## Goal
Turn the supplied Viger and Arcarna guidance into reusable, approved source material.

## Scope
- Inventory original supplied assets and flag anything reconstructed or awaiting confirmation.
- Define Viger corporate and Arcarna token sets: colours, typography, spacing, motion, logo usage and imagery rules.
- Create typed content models for products, feature chapters, pricing plans, comparisons, FAQs, contact routes, legal documents and company updates.
- Produce an editorial content matrix that links each page to an owner and source status.

## Acceptance checks
- Corporate and Arcarna tokens cannot be accidentally mixed by default.
- Every public price, entitlement and legal statement has an approval/source status.
- Components consume structured content rather than hard-coded prose where future editing is expected.
