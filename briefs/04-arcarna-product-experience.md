# Brief 04 — Arcarna Product Experience

## Goal
Build the flagship Arcarna sales story as a premium but accessible scroll-driven product experience.

## Scope
- Create scenes: Reveal, Problem, Question → Truth → Action, feature chapters (Sell, Understand, Control, Act, Grow), proof/comparison, pricing and conversion CTAs.
- Use real product imagery/content supplied by Viger; do not use generic stock imagery for product claims.
- Use targeted motion only: pinned scenes, cross-fades, charts and colour changes where they improve comprehension.
- Build a complete non-animated reduced-motion version with identical information and actions.

## Acceptance checks
- The experience is visually distinct from corporate Viger Cloud pages but retains clear ownership.
- It is keyboard usable, performant on mobile and functional with motion disabled.
- Every feature explains a business result before a technical mechanism.
