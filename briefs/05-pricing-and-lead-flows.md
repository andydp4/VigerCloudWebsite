# Brief 05 — Pricing and Lead Flows

## Goal
Create an editable pricing/comparison experience and safe, measurable routes for trials, demonstrations and enquiries.

## Scope
- Show Solo, Team, Growth and Scale plans using approved monthly/annual figures and wording that prices exclude VAT.
- Provide comparison tables, billing-cycle selection and consultation-only handling for larger plans.
- Implement short, accessible forms for trial, demo, general, pricing, partner, support, press and privacy requests.
- Validate server-side, protect against spam, retain consent/source/UTM data and route each enquiry by a defined contract.

## Acceptance checks
- No payment details are requested before onboarding suitability is understood.
- Form errors, success states and route types are clear and tested.
- External email/CRM delivery stays in test mode until Staging Gate approval.
