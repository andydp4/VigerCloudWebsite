# Brief 07 — Content Administration

## Goal
Enable authorised staff to safely manage marketing content without source-code edits.

## Scope
- Select and integrate the approved structured-content option (Sanity preferred; MDX acceptable for first release if editing needs remain simple).
- Support product records, feature chapters, imagery/video, plan availability, prices, comparisons, FAQs, legal versions, contact details, updates and redirects.
- Provide previews, draft/publish controls, restricted editor roles and audit history for price changes.
- Write a plain-English administration guide.

## Acceptance checks
- An authorised non-developer can update a product, price, comparison row and legal effective date safely in staging.
- Prices never exist only as unstructured page text.
