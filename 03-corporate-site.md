# Brief 03 — Corporate Website

## Goal
Build the Viger Cloud home, About, Products, Partners, Contact and shared corporate footer.

## Scope
- Implement clear corporate navigation and the approved sitemap.
- Home: Viger Cloud positioning, featured Arcarna, product ecosystem, audiences, responsible data/reliability, update area and contact CTA.
- About: group relationship, purpose, what Viger Cloud builds and who it serves.
- Products: premium product cards with honest status labels; no placeholder portfolio.
- Contact/Partners: distinct enquiry paths and transparent destination expectations.

## Acceptance checks
- Visitors can understand the offer and find the trial/demo route within one minute.
- Company attribution and statutory footer appear correctly.
- All pages work responsively and link into the Arcarna, pricing and legal journeys.
